Slide 1

Project Introduction

Osteoporosis is a bone disease characterized by decreased bone density, making bones weak and susceptible to fractures. Early prediction helps healthcare professionals identify high-risk individuals and begin preventive treatment.

Project Objectives
  • Predict osteoporosis risk using Machine Learning.
  • Analyze patient clinical data.
  • Compare multiple ML algorithms.
  • Select the best-performing model.
  • Develop a web application using Flask.
